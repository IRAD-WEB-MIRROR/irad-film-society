Join the Irad Film Society

Members pay for the next restoration. In return, the festival is yours.

Member
2,000 som a year: festival pass, Sunday morning screenings at the Oktyabr, the newsletter
Student
600 som a year, the same
Friend of a film
25,000 som: your name in the credits of the next restoration

Join at the Oktyabr box office, or on Sunday mornings in the foyer from 10:00. Bring a photo for the card.

Sunday mornings

Every Sunday from October to May the society shows a film from the archive at 10:30 in the Oktyabr’s small hall, with tea. Members free, guests 100 som.

Irad Film Society, a non-profit for the keeping and showing of Iradi films · Cinema Boulevard, Talas · festival@korkom.org
Screenings are held under the permits of the Department of Public Information. Children’s screenings and the 2027 restoration with the support of the Körköm Foundation.
