Every festival

The Irad Film Society began in 1993. Its festival has been held every other September since 1995. Seventeen festivals, counting 2027.

  • The Sunday club · 1993–1999
    Chair: Nurlan Sadikov.
  • The best year · 2000–2007
    Chair: Nurlan Sadikov.
  • Restorations · 2008–2017
    Chair: Beknur Karimov (2008–2018).
  • Guide of the Sands · 2018–2027
    Chair: Talgat Osmonov (since 2018).
