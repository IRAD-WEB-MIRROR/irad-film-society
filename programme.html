Programme 2027

Ten days, two halls, one restoration. The seventeenth Talas International Film Festival.

  1. Fri 10 SepOpening night: Guide of the Sands (1981), new restoration
    Introduced by Kadyrbek Asylbekov's daughter. 19:00, invitation and 400 som    Oktyabr
  2. Sat 11 SepVisions of Irad (1984), the 31 places, on a big screen again
    16:00    Oktyabr
  3. Sat 11 SepChildren's morning, free: Tulpar Tales and a short about horses
    11:00    Palace of Culture
  4. Sun 12 SepZarina (1968), the 2014 restoration
    12:00 and 18:00    Oktyabr
  5. Mon 13 SepRussian cinema today: four new films from Moscow and St Petersburg
    17:00 and 20:00, Russian    Palace of Culture
  6. Tue 14 SepThe Silver Horseshoe (1984): the comic Eastern, with the bookkeeper's grandson
    19:00    Oktyabr
  7. Wed 15 SepKazakh and Kyrgyz shorts
    18:00, with Russian subtitles    Palace of Culture
  8. Thu 16 SepVengeance Rider (1986), the 2023 restoration, with Lyudmila Tsoi in conversation
    19:00    Oktyabr
  9. Fri 17 SepHome movies of Talas: films people brought us, 1958 to 1991
    18:00, free    Oktyabr
  10. Sat 18 SepChildren's morning, free: cartoons
    11:00    Palace of Culture
  11. Sat 18 SepRise of Glory (1979), both parts with an interval
    14:00 to 19:00    Oktyabr
  12. Sun 19 SepClosing night: Russian premiere to be announced in August
    19:00, invitation and 400 som    Oktyabr

Box office

Venues

Oktyabr cinema, Cinema Boulevard: the premiere house of Talasfilm since the 1950s and the Talasfilm Archives’ cinematheque since 1996. 620 seats. Palace of Culture: the small hall, 240 seats, for the new films and the children.

The programme is subject to the screening permits, which are issued in August. Films may change.

Irad Film Society, a non-profit for the keeping and showing of Iradi films · Cinema Boulevard, Talas · festival@korkom.org
Screenings are held under the permits of the Department of Public Information. Children’s screenings and the 2027 restoration with the support of the Körköm Foundation.
